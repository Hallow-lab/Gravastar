Tutorial & FAQ

Quick start

  1. Check your Timetable tab — remove subjects you don't take, or add missing ones.
  2. Every day, open Mark Today and tick each period Present / Absent / OD / Medical.
  3. Your Dashboard updates instantly — overall %, till-date %, per-subject 75% status.
  4. Ask the floating AI button anything, in English or Tanglish.
How is "till date" % different from overall %?

Overall % is attended ÷ total classes for the whole semester so far. Till-date % looks only at the classes that have already happened between the dates you pick.

Does OD or Medical leave hurt my attendance?

No — OD and Medical-leave periods are excluded from both the attended and total count for that subject, so your percentage is recalculated instantly without penalty.

What triggers "Irreversible Detention"?

If even attending every single remaining class in a subject can't bring you back to 75%, Bunksite shows this alert for that subject.

Is my net ID password sent anywhere?

In this prototype, no data leaves your browser — everything is stored locally. A production version would need a secure backend before ever touching real credentials.
